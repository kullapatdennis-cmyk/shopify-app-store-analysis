# Analyst Memo — Shopify App Store Insights

**Dashboard:** Shopify App Store Analysis
**Reporting Period:** Latest available data (2018–2024)

## Key Insight
The number of reviews has been growing a lot, especially since 2022. This 
means more merchants are using and talking about the apps in the store. 
Looking at all 500 apps and about 8,000 reviews, the average rating is 
4.19 out of 5, which is a good score and shows that most merchants are 
happy with the apps. However, only 25% of reviews get a reply from the 
app developer. This means most merchants who leave a review don't hear 
back. The categories with the most reviews are SEO, Reviews & Ratings, 
and Sales & Conversion, so these are the areas merchants use and talk 
about the most.

## Business Impact
Since only 1 out of every 4 reviews gets a developer reply, merchants may 
feel like their feedback is being ignored. This could make them trust the 
app store less, especially in the categories that get the most reviews. 
Also, since the number of reviews is growing fast, it will become more 
important to keep up with replying to merchants and keeping app quality 
high as more people join.

## Recommendation
1. Ask or encourage app developers to reply to reviews more often, 
   especially in the busiest categories (SEO, Reviews & Ratings, Sales & 
   Conversion).
2. Keep an eye on the average rating over time. If reviews keep growing 
   but the rating starts to drop, that could mean quality is falling 
   behind growth.
3. Look into categories with fewer reviews (like Inventory Management and 
   Subscriptions) to understand if merchants just don't need those apps 
   as much, or if there's a chance to improve and grow that area.
